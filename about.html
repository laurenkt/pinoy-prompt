Bigkas · Pronunciation

Know exactly which sound to fix

Say a word and the coach lines up every sound you made against the Tagalog, then shows you how to move your mouth to get the one that's off.

"Walang anuman" (you're welcome), said two ways. Tap to hear each one.

  • Private. The speech model runs on your phone, so your voice never leaves it.
  • Precise. It hears sounds, not words, so it catches the near misses a word checker lets through, and the stress too.
  • Fair. Everyday and regional ways of saying a sound count as right.

Taglish · Real talk

Sound like Manila, not a phrasebook

Learn Taglish, po and opo, and the little words that carry a sentence. Everyday words come first, ranked by how often they turn up in Filipino film and TV.
